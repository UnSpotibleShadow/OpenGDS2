# Facilities and promotion

Company facilities occupy tenant plots. Construction and services are separate
purchases; read each confirmation before spending. Pending events can block a
new service until their conversation or receipt is acknowledged.

| Facility | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Game school | School/student and annual recruitment workflows. Automatic student skill growth is not established. |
| Staff hot spring | Staff care, with employee reactions. |
| Recycling shop | Dispose of eligible game inventory through its service. |
| Technology school | Employee technology-study service. |
| Casino | Coin purchase and the implemented casino games. |
| Law office | Fee-negotiation service and its business-meeting flow. |
| Mystery lab | Experiments with source-backed random outcomes and saved progress/results. |
| Production lab | Paid permanent production-capacity increases. |
| Hardware lab | Own-console development and launch workflow. |

Production lab service costs **¥10,000,000** and adds **15–19 production points**
per successful use. The recovered increment does not grow with company wealth
or year. Its permanence and its fixed increment are different concepts.

Mystery-lab experiments are random and can be harmful. Read the result, and do
not assume every experiment improves your company. Hardware development costs
and steps are separate from developing software for an existing platform.

## Promotion

The six promotion media have their own presentations/results. Availability
depends on project state; read the offered action and its cost. Promotion is a
paid project action, not a permanent employee ability or a guaranteed sales total.

## Casino quirks

Poker, blackjack, card-chain and horse racing retain documented original quirks.
Blackjack ace scoring/payout retention and horse pair-wager handling differ from
what you might expect from modern casino games; horse results can display
winnings that the original next-race path discards. Read the in-game explanation
before betting. These modes are not a safe money-generation guarantee.
