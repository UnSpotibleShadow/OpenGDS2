# Employees and technologies

## Permanent employee growth

Employees earn experience through supported work. Spend it through the employee's
**Level up** action when eligible; the result shows the actual stat gains. Those
saved employee improvements can benefit later projects. The recovered ordinary
ability-growth path caps affected abilities at **99**; level-up gains and other
costs depend on the employee and current level.

Stress affects availability. Give employees rest and review the secretary's
rest/production policies. A different secretary changes the identity/artwork;
a separate passive game-quality bonus has not been established.

## Purchase, train, then use

These are different steps:

1. **Company purchase:** buy a technology in Technologies to make training available.
2. **Employee training:** select the employee, right-click a study workstation,
   then open the employee’s technology view and click **Learn**, beside **Use**.
   Choose a course and confirm **Learn**.
   Training requires experience, available technology levels and learning capacity.
3. **Application:** return the employee to a development desk in the intended
   project room and select the learned technology when eligible.

Training completion raises that employee's learned level, up to level 3, and
ends the training task. Check/reassign their development desk to resume project
work; a completed training task is not an automatic application to a game.
Special recruits can have prelearned technology without a separate company purchase.

**Learned technologies are not blanket passive quality bonuses.** Applying one
spends experience. Supported applications increase fun, novelty, graphics or
music, reduce bugs or data size, relieve stress, or affect ordinary work speed.
Speed does not accelerate debugging. A quality percentage increase on zero
still leaves zero; repeated quality/compression/bug applications have diminished
benefits on the same project.

Do not repeatedly replace an in-progress application: it spends experience again
and has a documented stall risk. Reassignment resets the relevant work behavior.
Some older saves lack eligibility/state information and are guarded before
spending. Level-three stress relief retains the recovered actor-slot-0 targeting
quirk; it is not a generic boost for the whole staff.
