# Your first game

## Create your studio

After importing the original data, enter your company and president names and
choose a mode. **Beginner** ends after Year 2, December, week 4. **Normal** is
experimental ongoing play. Either mode dissolves the company if funds go negative.

Hire employees and assign them to development desks. Their room assignments
matter: technology applications target the project in the employee's room.
Keep enough cash for payroll, contracts, production and unexpected events.

## Plan and develop

1. Start development in a free room.
2. Choose eligible hardware and an audience. Obtain a hardware license when required.
3. Review the planning proposal, focus and cost, then begin development.
4. Work through the project's meetings and available content/genre choices.
5. Watch quality, bugs, data size and development progress. Read cost confirmations
   before accepting paid choices or promotions.

Original games, eligible Hall of Fame sequels and manga adaptations have distinct
entry routes. Do not assume an ordinary game is automatically sequel-eligible.

## Debug and release

Reaching full development progress does not mean every release concern is solved.
Check bugs, media capacity, production cost and cash before shipping. Choose a
media option that fits the project's size. Set a title, retail price and initial
production quantity that you can afford.

The release screen shows costs and expected receipts. Those projections do not
guarantee that every copy will sell. Remaining stock ties up money and can also
increase a [recall charge](Consumer-Investigations).

Read the magazine reviews, follow sales and produce more copies when useful.
The entire studio benefits from managing staff growth, assignments and rest;
[technologies](Employees-and-Technologies) must be learned and actively applied.

Dialogs and meetings can suspend simulation. Read their remaining pages and
acknowledge results before assuming the clock is stuck. Save regularly and keep
an external backup before upgrading the game.
