# Sales, reviews and awards

The Products window lists released games. Open a game's sales window for its
weekly sales history, cumulative copies sold, remaining stock and production
controls. **Stock** is unsold inventory; cumulative sales and the weekly chart
measure different things.

Producing more copies spends money before those copies sell. Reprinting can be
blocked by cash, the shared production/budget guard or a recall result. A disabled
button does not always mean the game has a missing feature; inspect its state
and any pending event first.

Magazine reviews show reviewer scores and comments. English reviewer/game names
are display translations; saved identities and player-entered titles are preserved.
A review score does not promise a particular sales total.

Annual awards use a conversation/reel flow and category results. Read the remaining
announcement pages before expecting office time to resume. Hall of Fame eligibility
is separate from ordinary release or a good review and affects eligible sequels.

The Company window groups recognition/capacity/library information, released and
Hall of Fame counts, the highest-selling released game, period profits and three
tenants. Weekly/monthly/yearly profit values are period accounting, not your
current cash balance.

A consumer investigation may arrive after demand has fallen. **A successful game
can still generate a large recall liability.** Read [Consumer investigations](Consumer-Investigations)
before treating accumulated sales as risk-free cash.
