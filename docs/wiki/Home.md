# Open Game Dev Story II

Welcome to the player guide for **OpenGDS2**, an unofficial reconstruction of the
original PC **Game Dev Story 2 DX**, built with Godot and C#.

Run a studio, hire and train employees, develop and ship games, and follow their
reviews and sales. English and Japanese interfaces are available. Early Access
is playable but incomplete: expect bugs, presentation differences and missing
or unverified original behavior.

complete original-game parity. Original files are required and imported locally;
they are not distributed with OpenGDS2. Original-game saves cannot be imported.
