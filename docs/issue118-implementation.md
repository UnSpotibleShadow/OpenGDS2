with a saved next-room cursor. Active software rooms with known history consume
the eager trigger draw, including rooms failing awareness/stage/already-interviewed

recognition >15, stage ≥2 and a clear `A129C8` flag.

and save validators reject conflicting events, invalid cursors and altered gains.
